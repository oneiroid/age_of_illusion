# ChevronRule

A full-width band of zigzag floor stripes in `floor-light` over `floor-dark`, used under a red band or between major regions.

- Provide only `pitch`: `s` (28px) between sections, `l` (48px) under a hero or above a footer.
- At most twice per page. Place it directly beneath a `curtain` field when possible, as floor under drapes.
- Decorative and hidden from assistive technology. No text on it.
