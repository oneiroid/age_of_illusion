# MirrorPanel

A two-sided panel split by a silver axis with a black spike, for paired values: before and after, expected and actual, you and your double.

- Provide `left` and `right` with the same structure. The left side aligns to the axis from the right, the right side from the left.
- Make one detail on the right differ: a later time, a missing word, the `lamp` stamp instead of the neutral one. The panel shows the difference by symmetry.
- `label` names the comparison for screen readers. `lit` adds `lamp-glow`; one lit element per screen.
