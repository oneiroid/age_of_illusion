# Textures

Four treatments of one bilaterally symmetric monochrome print with a central black spike. Show them through `Veil` and keep the spike on the vertical centre.

- `mirror-blood.webp`: duotone from black through `curtain-deep` and `curtain` to `lamp` highlights. Default backdrop in Lodge.
- `mirror-band.webp`: wide crop of mirror-blood around the spike, for headers and narrow bands.
- `mirror-print.webp`: the original greyscale print, darkened, with vignette. Use in Insomnia.
- `mirror-negative.webp`: the inverted print. Afterimages, the right side of a doubled pair, pages reached after an error.
