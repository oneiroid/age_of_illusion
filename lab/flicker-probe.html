Flicker probe

For Your Eyes Only alternates two pre-drawn frames with every screen refresh. A dropped or repeated frame makes one colour last longer than the other: a brief flash of brightness that everyone sees. This page swaps two identical grey layers the same way for 10 seconds and records the time between frames. Nothing visible flickers. Keep the tab in front and the mouse still while it runs.

Go / no-go from the plan: fewer than 1 dropped frame per 200. Then calibrate on this screen and check by eye that the word disappears at 50 cm. Back to the demo.
